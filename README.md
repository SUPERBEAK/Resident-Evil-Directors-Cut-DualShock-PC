DX12 native port of RE 1 DC dualshock. 

Based on https://github.com/ThePerfectVale/Resident-Evil-Directors-Cut-Dualshock-Decompilation

You can walk through 90% of the map. 

Textures, menus, puzzles, scripting, all need doing. Primary mechanics are being implemented. 

This is the best version of the game, including the soundtrack. Don't argue with me about it. 

Eventually, this will include mod support, asset swaps, scripting engine, randomizer. 

You need to provide your own ROM.
